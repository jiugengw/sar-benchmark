# Unsubmitted ZIPs

Prediction ZIPs that were packaged but never uploaded to Codabench, grouped by packaging date (`DDMMYY`, Singapore time) and kept with
their metadata. Uploaded ZIPs and their official scores are in [`../submissions/`](../submissions/README.md). What
each experiment was, and what it taught us, is in [`../docs/HISTORY.md`](../docs/HISTORY.md).
